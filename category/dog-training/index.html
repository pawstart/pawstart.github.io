Dog Training Basics

You don't need to be a professional trainer to raise a well-behaved dog — you need a clear plan and realistic expectations. This section covers the fundamentals every new dog owner actually needs: crate training, house training, leash manners, recall, and solving common problems like biting, barking, and separation anxiety. Our approach is force-free and beginner-friendly: short sessions, clear steps, and honest timelines (no, your puppy won't be trained in a weekend). Where behavior problems can have medical causes, we say so plainly and point you to your veterinarian — because good training starts with a healthy dog. Work through these guides in any order; each one stands alone and tells you exactly what to do, day by day. Expect setbacks — they're a normal part of learning, not a sign you're doing it wrong. Consistency beats perfection every time, and the dogs who learn fastest are simply the ones whose owners kept showing up.
