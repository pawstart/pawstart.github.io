Cat Care Basics

Cats are famously independent, but first-time cat owners still have plenty to learn: litter box setup that actually works, scratching solutions that save your furniture, how to introduce a new cat to your home without chaos, and enrichment ideas that keep indoor cats happy on a budget. These guides respect how cats really behave instead of fighting it — we show you how to work with their instincts, not against them. Every recommendation is practical and tested in real homes, and where health is involved we cite veterinary sources and remind you that we're researchers and pet owners, not vets. Whether you've adopted a tiny kitten or a dignified senior, you'll find a calm, structured starting point here. And because cats hide discomfort well, we're explicit about the line between behavior you can work on at home and signs that warrant a vet visit — knowing the difference is one of the most valuable skills a new cat owner can build.
