Healthy Habits

A healthy pet starts with good everyday habits: vaccination schedules you can actually follow, grooming basics, exercise needs, and knowing when something warrants a vet visit. This section covers preventive care and daily routines — the unglamorous stuff that keeps pets thriving for years. An important boundary, stated plainly: we are pet-owner researchers, not veterinarians. You’ll find zero diagnosis, zero treatment advice, and zero medication guidance here — those decisions belong to you and your vet. What you will find is well-sourced general information, clear explanations of what vets recommend and why, and prominent reminders to consult your veterinarian whenever your pet’s health is in question. When in doubt, call your vet first and read our articles second. Prevention is quieter and cheaper than treatment — these small, consistent habits are the highest-leverage care you’ll ever give. Small steps, done consistently, beat occasional grand gestures. Start with one habit this week.
