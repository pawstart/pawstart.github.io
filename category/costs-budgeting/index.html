Costs & Budgeting

Pets are family — and like family, they cost real money. This section gives you honest, sourced numbers: what a puppy or kitten really costs in the first year, how pet insurance actually works (and whether it's worth it for you), and how to feed and care for your pet well without overspending. We break costs down per day, per month, and per year, and we show our sources so you can check our math. No sponsored rankings, no inflated scare figures, no suspiciously cheap promises — just transparent budgeting help so money never becomes the reason a pet doesn't get the care they need. Prices are checked against current retailer and veterinary data, and every article is dated so you know how fresh the numbers are. If a figure looks surprising, check the cited source — transparency is the whole point, and we'd rather you verify than trust. Bookmark the guides you use — updated prices beat memory every time.
