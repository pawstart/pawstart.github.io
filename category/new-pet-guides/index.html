New Pet Guides

Bringing home a dog or cat for the first time is exciting — and a little overwhelming. These guides walk you through the first days and weeks, step by step: what to buy before your pet arrives, how to set up your home, what the first vet visit looks like, and how long it really takes a new pet to settle in. Everything here is written for absolute beginners. We explain terms instead of assuming you know them, we tell you what matters most first, and every factual claim is traced to a cited source — veterinary schools, official organizations, and product documentation. If you're starting from zero, start here: pick the guide that matches your situation (puppy, kitten, or rescue), follow it in order, and you'll give your new companion the calm, prepared welcome they deserve. Bookmark this section and return as questions come up — most new owners revisit these guides several times in the first month, and that's exactly what they're for.
